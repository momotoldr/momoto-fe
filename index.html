Get in the Same Frame, Even Miles Apart

Momoto is a little online studio for couples and friends who live apart. Step into a live photobooth together, then gather your strips into a scrapbook. It's how we stayed close across the distance, and we hope it does the same for you.

Things to do together

  • Virtual Photobooth — Take four synced shots on your own, with your date or with the whole group, then style your strip with filters, stickers and new backgrounds.
  • Scrapbook — Fill a little book with strips from your gallery and your own words, then keep it as a PDF.
